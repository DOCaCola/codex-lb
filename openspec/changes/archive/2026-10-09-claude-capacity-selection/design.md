## Decisions
Keep validation/authorization and request projection before admission, as required by shared source dispatch. Atomic try-claim remains authoritative: do not add a racy capacity precheck. A typed local Claude capacity rejection returns to the request-wide selection loop, excludes that candidate, and reprepares the logical request. A candidate is visited at most once for capacity rejection. No physical-send budget is consumed. Existing preparation enforces opaque ownership and same-account retry pinning; exclusions cannot override either.

Acquired claims retain the existing SourceDispatch ownership and cleanup path. No new queue or claim held during credential refresh. Repreparation preserves native session semantics; soft affinity may rebind, resource ownership cannot. Exhaustion returns local 503 model_source_busy with Retry-After: 1 and no quota/auth/health changes.

Native affinity publication moves after admission. Otherwise a rejected new candidate would incorrectly become a retained owner, and subsequent capacity reselection would create a resource-ambiguity marker despite no upstream send. A frozen pending binding stores the observed owner, rechecks it, then uses the existing compare-and-swap claim. An incompatible concurrent binding returns retryable claude_session_changed before inference, releasing the admission slot; no account guess or unbounded preparation loop.

Reuse ModelSource.max_concurrency, expose null or positive integer in Claude settings. Limits are per worker, not global subscription limits. Null remains unlimited. Do not infer provider limits or add RPM/session-count settings. Inspired by Sub2API selection/slot integration and CLIProxyAPI typed local-busy separation; not copied code.
